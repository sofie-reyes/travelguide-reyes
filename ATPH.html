About the Philippines

; Scattered like emeralds across the western Pacific Ocean, the Philippines is a breathtaking archipelago of over 7,600 islands divided into three main geographic regions: Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. Positioned along the Pacific Ring of Fire, this tropical paradise boasts dramatic natural contrasts, from the iconic, symmetrical cone of Mount Mayon and the ancient, hand-carved Banaue Rice Terraces to limestone cliffs rising sheer out of turquoise lagoons in Palawan. Travelers can explore powdery white-sand beaches, dive into the heart of the Coral Triangle—the global center of marine biodiversity—or trek through lush volcanic highlands and subterranean rivers. Whether you are island-hopping in the Visayas or chasing waterfalls in Mindanao, the Philippines’ wild, sun-drenched geography promises endless adventure at every turn.
